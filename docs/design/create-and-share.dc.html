How it works

Four steps, about two minutes end to end.

1
Add the options
Type a name or paste a link. Anyone you invite can throw theirs in too.
2
Share one link
Drop it in the group chat. Nobody needs an app, an account, or a password to vote.
3
Everyone ranks
Drag your top three. Each person gets one veto for a hard no.
4
The timer decides
When it runs out the top-ranked place wins, and everyone sees it at the same moment.
Good to know
·Votes are anonymous. Everyone sees totals, never who picked what.
·Only the organizer can nudge or close voting early.
·Change your ranking any time before the timer ends.
