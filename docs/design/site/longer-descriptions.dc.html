300 wide · drawn at full scroll length
Type. Description: Instrument Sans 400, 12px / 1.4 (16.8px lines), clamped to 4 lines with an ellipsis, #3B4A42 (picked tile: same colour on mint). Vetoed: #5E6D64, still 5.6:1 on white. Address stays DM Mono 400 10px, one line, ellipsis. Name 700 13px / 1.15, unchanged.
Why 4, not 3. At 300 the tile's text column is about 103px, roughly 16–17 characters a line. Three lines shows about a third of a 140-character description; four shows close to half, which is enough to tell options apart. The tallest cell (photo, a 2-line name, address, 4 lines, bar) is about 245px. On a 600px-tall screen that's about one and a half rows above the Send button, so the grid scrolls as before.
Veto used. Three signals once the one veto is spent. A tangerine pill under the title reads VETO USED · BAVEL. Every other VETO bar turns dashed and grey, with no shadow and a hollow ✕ ring, and is disabled; its label says your veto is on Bavel. The vetoed tile's own bar stays solid tangerine and reads VETOED, and tapping it undoes the veto, which brings the other bars back. The count line under the grid reads 0 VETOES LEFT, in the pattern of the old 1 VETO LEFT.
Even rows. Each grid cell is a column: tile on top (it grows to fill the row), then the VETO + More row pinned at the bottom, so bars line up across a row whatever the text length. No tile reserves a description slot: a row of two short tiles (Night + Market, Sushi Enya) shrinks to fit. When a tile without a description shares a row with a long one (Kismet beside Guisados), the extra height sits as plain white at the foot of the tile, inside its tap area, and not as an empty text box.
Hit areas. Tile, VETO and More are three separate buttons with no overlap: 6px of dead space between the tile and the bar row, 6px between VETO and More. More is 44×44 visible, so no invisible padding is needed; VETO is the rest of the row, about 79×44 at 300.
Departures. Phones are 300 wide like t1–t7; the 02 and 03 lists are 340 like t7-e. No earlier frame draws a per-tile VETO bar on the sticker grid (t7-f has none), so this bar is new, built from the brief: white sticker bar with a tangerine ✕ dot; vetoed it fills tangerine and reads VETOED in ink (6.6:1). At 79px the bar has no room for “· UNDO”, so undo lives in the bar's accessible label and in the t8-a note. The brief names Inter, but every frame in this file uses Instrument Sans for body text, so the description uses Instrument Sans to keep the system unchanged. The vetoed tile turns off picking and fades its photo stripes and chip border, not the chip text.

Behaviour. The one way to close is the ✕ button (44×44) top right. Type. Description: Instrument Sans 400, 14px / 1.45 (20.3px lines), #3B4A42, clamped to 4 lines with an ellipsis (was 2). Name 700 21px; chip + address unchanged from t7-f (11.5px).
The clamp at 300. The card's text column is about 224px, roughly 34 characters a line, so a 140-character description runs just over 4 lines and the last few words go to an ellipsis (drawn). Anything up to about 130 characters shows in full.
Reads as a button. More is a 44px pill with the 2px ink border and offset shadow, the sticker-button shape that nothing else on the card uses. It sits on its own row under the text, so it doesn't touch the photo, which is the main area people grab to drag. A tap on More opens the t8-c sheet. A press on More that moves more than ~10px turns into a drag and doesn't open the sheet. Mid-drag the pill rotates with the card but stays opaque white on top, and it keeps full contrast. Any yes/no tint added later has to go under it.
Departures. The photo gives up height to the 2 extra lines and the More row; the card frame and stack are the same as t7-f (300 wide), with the crop 80px taller to hold them.
